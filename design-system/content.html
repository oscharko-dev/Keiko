Content & Voice

Keiko speaks the way it looks: precise, calm and quietly confident. It explains, never shouts; it earns trust by being clear about what it knows and what it did. Ex experientia disco — it learns from experience, and so does its writing.

01 — VOICE

Four traits

Precise

Say the exact thing. Name the file, the count, the action. No vague reassurance.

Calm

Steady at all times — especially in errors. No alarm, no exclamation marks.

Governed

Transparent about scope, sources and consequences. Trust over hype.

Plain

Short words, present tense, second person. Explain jargon or drop it.

02 — MICROCOPY

Write it like this

The difference between calm and noisy is usually a few words.

Do
New window
Verb + noun. The user knows exactly what happens.
Per-chat source limit reached.
States the fact plainly, in context.
Open a window to start working
Empty state points to the next step.
Connected · src/
Shows scope; the user always knows what’s in play.
Don’t
Create a brand-new window now!
Padding and an exclamation mark add noise, not meaning.
Oops! Something went wrong 😬
Cute, vague, unhelpful — and emoji aren’t the brand.
Nothing here yet…
Dead end with no next action.
Successfully connected!!!
Over-celebrates a routine event.
03 — TERMINOLOGY

Say this, not that

One word per concept, used consistently across UI, docs and errors.

Use Not Note
Window tab, pane Cards on the canvas are windows.
Workspace desktop, board The whole canvas.
Grounding context mode What an answer is based on.
Source attachment, doc A connected folder, repo or file.
Connect link, attach Joining a window to a source.
Full screen maximise, expand The green window control.
MemoriaViva memory, history Product name — never translated.
04 — MECHANICS

Mechanics

Case Sentence case for everything — buttons, titles, menus. Reserve ALL-CAPS for tiny tracked labels.
Punctuation No exclamation marks. Periods on full sentences; none on labels, buttons or single-line hints.
Person & tense Second person, present tense: “Open a window”, not “A window will be opened”.
Numbers & units Numerals for counts (3 windows); thin space before units (1 200 tokens · 100%).
Emoji Not part of the brand. Use a Lift icon instead.
05 — AI DISCLOSURE & PRIVACY

Writing for the agent

Agentic copy carries extra duty: say what the agent is doing, what it remembers, what it can't be sure of, and what leaves the device. Plain, present-tense, no euphemisms.

Do
Reading 3 files in src/ to answer.
Names the action and the scope as it happens.
Based on your last 5 chats in this workspace.
Memory disclosure — what it drew on, and the boundary.
I'm not certain — verify against the source.
Names low confidence and points to grounding.
This sends your selection to the model provider.
States plainly what leaves the device, before it does.
Don't
Working on it…
Hides what the agent is actually touching.
I remember everything about you.
Vague and faintly menacing; states no boundary.
Here's the answer.
Projects false certainty on a low-confidence result.
Don't worry about your data.
Reassurance instead of disclosure — erodes trust.
Moment Pattern Why
Acting “[Verb]-ing [object] in [scope]” — present continuous, named target. The user can always see what the agent touches.
Memory “Based on [what], in [boundary].” Offer “Forget this”. Discloses recall and gives control.
Uncertainty “I'm not certain — [how to verify].” Pair with the confidence component. Hallucination risk is named, not hidden.
Privacy “This sends [what] to [where].” Before the send, not after. Consent is informed and timely.
Permission “Allow [scope]?” with the exact grants listed. Matches the AI permission component.
