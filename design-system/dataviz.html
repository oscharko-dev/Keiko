Grouped & stacked

03 — TOOLTIP & UNCERTAINTY

Readout & the unknown

Tooltips name the series with a swatch and a tabular value. Uncertain or estimated data is hatched — never just a paler colour, which fails for colour-blind readers and in high-contrast.
