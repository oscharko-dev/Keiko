03 — RULES

Contribution & deprecation

How a change enters the system, and how one leaves it.

To contribute
1
Resolve to existing tokens. A change that needs a new colour, hue or one-off value is a token proposal first.
2
Ship the full template — states & a11y included — before status can move to Ready.
3
An area owner reviews; the changelog entry is part of the change, not an afterthought.
To deprecate
1
Mark Deprecated with a named replacement and the version it lands in.
2
Keep it working for one minor cycle; emit nothing breaking.
3
Remove only after the migration note shows zero internal consumers.
