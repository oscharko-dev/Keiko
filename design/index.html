Keiko · Design System

One hand, everywhere.

The shared language behind Keiko’s surface — tokens, type, components, motion and the rules that keep them consistent. Built on the real globals.css, so what you read here is what ships. Dark & light, accessible by default.

Monochrome & calm

Grey-white does the work. Colour — the orca green — only speaks on interaction or status.

Governed & reproducible

Every choice is a token or a rule. New work in three months or three years still matches.

Accessible by default

WCAG-tuned contrast, visible focus, full keyboard reach, reduced-motion respected — in both themes.
