Guidelines · 05

Motion

Motion is feedback, not decoration. It’s quick, quiet and earns its place — confirming a state change or guiding the eye. Every animation has a still fallback for prefers-reduced-motion.

01 — PRINCIPLES

Three rules

Fast & subtle

120–180ms, gentle easing. If you notice it consciously, it’s too much.

Meaningful

Motion shows a state change — hover, focus, data flowing. Never loops for decoration on content.

Optional

All motion is gated behind reduced-motion, which swaps to an instant or static state.

02 — TIMING & EASING

Durations

A short scale. Hover/colour shifts are near-instant; entrances are a touch slower; only the data-flow loops.

04 — THE CONNECTION FLOW

Data flow

When a connected source is being read, its edge carries a flowing dash. Heavier exchange flows faster and denser. Reduced-motion users get a distinct static dash instead of movement.

.conn-path[data-active] — flowing dash + particles when active; [data-intensity="heavy"] speeds it up. Always paired with a flow tag, so it never relies on motion or colour alone.

05 — REDUCED MOTION

Respecting reduced motion

Honoured
When the OS requests reduced motion, the design system sets all transitions/animations to ~0ms and swaps looping effects for a clear static state (e.g. the flow becomes a distinct dashed line). Nothing essential is conveyed by motion alone — try it via your OS setting and these demos go still.
